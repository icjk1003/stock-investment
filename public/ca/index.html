How are dividends handled?

We apply net dividends on dividend dates, and if DRIP is enabled, dividends are used to buy more shares.

Do I need FX rates?

For Canada, FX can matter if you invest in USD assets from CAD (depends on your display/policy settings).

QQQ has low dividends—still useful?

Yes. Even with low dividends, DCA performance is driven mainly by price changes and total return.
